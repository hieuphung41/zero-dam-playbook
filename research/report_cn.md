# CN slice report - Zero Dam (零号大坝), S11

Prepared 2026-10-31 by the replacement agent. Data limits up front: Bilibili AI subtitles were empty (login needed), most video descriptions are blank, and top comments are thin. Few S11-dated CN articles exist. Most of what I found is **pre-S11** or has no stated season, and it is marked as such.

## CN season
- CN S11 is "群星赛季" (Stellar), live from **2026-09-04**. Sources: Gamersky patch notes, https://www.gamersky.com/handbook/202609/2200664.shtml (2026-09-01) and Tencent News, https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260817A0ASHG00 (2026-08-17).
- The same season applies to the whole Sep-Oct window. Video titles carry "S11群星新赛季", and the 233乐园 page mentions "11.5新赛季 回声赛季", so the next CN season starts 2026-11-05. Many Oct videos say "S12超前爆料".
- The CN season number matches global S11. The start date is 4 days earlier (09-04 vs the brief's 09-08).
- Neither the Gamersky patch notes nor the Tencent News article mention any Zero Dam spawn, extraction or contract change. S11 added fishing at Zero Dam and a 1v1 custom room on the Admin map. The only change I could confirm is the Raven lamps (see Loot).

### Q4 Real rotations
Strong caveat. The only dated spawn/route article is 18183 from 2026-07-24 (pre-S11). It uses a different spawn naming (游客中心下方/上方, 坝顶, 东楼×2 一号位, 西楼×2 一号位, 水泥厂), and I cannot map it onto W1-E7. S11 CN videos name "西楼一号位", "东楼一号位", "水泥厂一号位", "变电站一号位" and "工地出生". The brief's #1 pairs are W4/W5 and E1/E4, so this matches loosely.

18183 article, 2026-07-24, UNCERTAIN (pre-S11, SINGLE):
- Visitor Center area spawns: if a big safe has spawned at the Visitor Center, skip it. Route: Barracks → Main Substation → Small Substation → Underground tunnel → Cement Plant, extract there.
- Crest spawn: if there is a cutscene, go up into the bus for gold bars and keycards. If not, take the zipline, then Parking → Container yard → Barracks → pipeline extract.
- East Wing (东楼) spawn: check whether the main-building safe is in the East Wing. If yes, clear it, then go to the Construction site. If not, check whether the Admin cement truck is still turning.
- Cement Plant spawn: loot in place, then Underground tunnel → Substation loot → Barracks → Visitor Center extract.
- Advice: "撤离别走行政楼中间 (易被架枪), 优先走西侧靠工地"; "7-8分钟拉极限闸撤离"; preferred extracts: west-south temporary extract and the crest.

S11 evidence on squad behaviour:
- BV14ynC69EUG (2026-10-22, Normal): the player says the west side "复活了四队" and that the riverbank squad "是西楼一号位直接冲水泥厂并且开局30秒不到就在水泥厂清小兵然后开局两分半被灭队". So the 西楼一号位 squad rushes the Cement Plant, and four squads on the west is suspicious to the author. SINGLE, anecdotal.
- BV1smxk6hE3u (斐延, 10-31): "变电站一号位" solo route in Normal. Comment: "跑刀去行政楼总被迫夺舍和猛攻" (running to the Admin building keeps forcing fights). SINGLE.
- Spawn-rule check: the claim "同侧1号点仅刷一队" and "14 spawns, 7 per side" is repeated in a search summary of a CN article I did not open. UNCERTAIN, consistent with what you already know.

### Q6 Loot and tricks
- **Raven lamps (惊喜渡鸦), S11, CONFIRMED by 18183 (2026-08-30) plus several S11 videos.** Four fixed lamps on Zero Dam: (1) outer plaza of the Visitor Center (游客中心外围广场); (2) under the flower bed below the Admin corridor, near the container yard (行政楼连廊下方花坛, 靠近集装箱); (3) river bank, beside a big tree opposite the red container (河滩, 红色集装箱对面大树旁); (4) the large open area left of the Cement Plant, by the yellow box (水泥厂左侧大空地, 黄色箱子边上). https://m.18183.com/gonglue/202608/2gilrbs7.html. Videos: 渡鸦 玩法 (BV1dSnW6UEiB, 10-22; BV1QynB6EEyo, 10-21). Player comment: Raven is often a trap ("渡鸦骗局", BV1vWHV6SEca).

- **Outdoor energy-tool spawns:** armoured-car battery, micro reactor, power pack, fusion unit, military shells, carbon-fibre plate, all in the open (BV1kinG6fEv5, 2026-10-18), description only.
- **Keycard rooms / red-loot spots:** Bilibili titles name loot routes, all S11: Visitor Center and Cement Plant (冬枣T, 09-01 to 09-08); Main Substation (09-04); Admin 1F and underground passage (10-01); "丝绸之路" (10-16); "全区域刷红点位路线" (10-02). UNCERTAIN.
